# Embedded Hardware Design

> [!info] Course navigation
> [[Embedded Systems Overview|Subject overview]] · [[Embedded Systems Roadmap|Course roadmap]] · [[Lecture-07.pdf|Lecture 7 overview]] · [[Lecture-Week-07.pdf|Week 7 consolidation]] · Previous: [[Embedded Digital Communications]] · Next: [[Software Engineering for Embedded Systems]]

## Core Ideas

- Reading and drawing schematics: power rails, ground, reference designators, and net labels.
- Power design: regulation, decoupling/bypass capacitors, and current budgeting for peripherals.
- I/O interfacing: pull-up/pull-down resistors, current limiting, level considerations, and driving loads (LEDs, motors, relays) through transistors.
- Signal integrity basics: debouncing switches, protecting inputs, and managing noise.
- Design for manufacture and test: connectors, headers, and testability of the board.
