## Course structure

ENME601 applies the First and Second Laws to open and closed systems, engineering devices, Brayton and Rankine power cycles, and vapour-compression refrigeration. Canvas describes in-person lectures, tutorials, and practical labs, with roughly six hours of weekly self-study beyond timetabled classes. Tutorial problems should be attempted before class. Attendance at the saturation and Rankine steam-cycle labs is compulsory.

## Current Week 1–3 sequence

- [Week 1](https://canvas.aut.ac.nz/courses/23175/pages/week-1) introduces systems, control volumes, properties, state, equilibrium, processes, cycles, temperature, and pressure. The current Page provides Chapter 1, topic slides, tutorial questions and solutions, property tables, and extra notes.
- [Week 2](https://canvas.aut.ac.nz/courses/23175/pages/week-2) covers forms of energy, heat and work transfer, the First Law, and the ideal-gas equation of state. The current Page provides Chapter 2, Topic 02, tutorial questions, and solutions.
- [Week 3](https://canvas.aut.ac.nz/courses/23175/pages/week-3) covers properties of pure substances, phase identification, property-table lookup and interpolation, quality, and ideal-gas applications. The 25 July announcement also identifies Week 3 as the first **Saturation Temperature and Pressure Lab** session and points to both the lab and apparatus manuals.
