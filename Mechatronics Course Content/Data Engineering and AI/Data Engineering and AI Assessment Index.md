## Lab-performance component

Each project phase allocates 25 marks to the report and 5 marks to lab performance. Phase 1 lab marks span Labs 2–6 and Phase 2 spans Labs 7–11, at one mark per week. Teaching assistants assess performance and exercise results during scheduled labs; the announcement explicitly says the marks are not based on merely submitting lab files.
