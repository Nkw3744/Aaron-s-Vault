## Core Ideas

- Locate every link and point of a mechanism given the link lengths and the input angle.
- Build the closed vector loop equation and separate it into real ($x$) and imaginary ($y$) scalar equations.
- Solve the four-bar in closed form (Freudenstein constants $K_1$--$K_5$) for the coupler angle $\theta_3$ and output angle $\theta_4$.
- Solve the offset slider-crank for $\theta_3$ and the slider position $d$.
- Distinguish the open ($-$) and crossed ($+$) assembly modes.
- Evaluate the transmission angle $\mu$ (law of cosines) and find toggle / motion-limiting positions and quick-return timing ratios.
