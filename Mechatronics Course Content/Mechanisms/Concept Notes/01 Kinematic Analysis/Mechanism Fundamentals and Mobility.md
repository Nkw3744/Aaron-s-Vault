## Core Ideas

- Distinguish machines, mechanisms, links, and joints.
- Represent a physical mechanism as a kinematic diagram suitable for analysis.
- Identify common link and joint types and determine the mobility of a planar mechanism.
- Use the Grashof criterion to classify four-bar linkages and predict which links can rotate fully.
- Establish the terminology and sign conventions used throughout position, velocity, and acceleration analysis.
