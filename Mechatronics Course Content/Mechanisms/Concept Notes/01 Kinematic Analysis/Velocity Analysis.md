## Core Ideas

- Differentiate the position vector loop once with respect to time; the factor $j$ rotates each velocity $90^\circ$ ahead of its position vector.
- Solve the four-bar for angular velocities $\omega_3$ and $\omega_4$ (requires $\theta_3,\theta_4$ from position analysis).
- Find point velocities with the relative-velocity (difference) equation $\mathbf{V}_B = \mathbf{V}_A + \mathbf{V}_{BA}$, and the coupler point $\mathbf{V}_P$.
- Solve the slider-crank for $\omega_3$, slider velocity $\dot d$, and the velocity of slip at a sliding joint.
- Locate instant centres using Kennedy's theorem ($N = n(n-1)/2$ centres).
