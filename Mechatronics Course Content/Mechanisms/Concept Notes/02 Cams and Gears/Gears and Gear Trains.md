## Core Ideas

- Involute tooth geometry: pitch, module/diametral pitch, pressure angle, AGMA full-depth tooth dimensions.
- Length of action $Z$ and contact ratio $m_p$ (require $m_p \ge 1.2$); interference and minimum tooth numbers.
- Velocity and torque ratios: $m_V = \mp N_{in}/N_{out}$ (external $-$, internal $+$), $m_T = 1/m_V$.
- Simple, compound, and reverted gear trains; overall ratio $= \pm \prod N_{drv}/\prod N_{drn}$.
- Planetary (epicyclic) trains via the relative-to-arm (two-step) method.
