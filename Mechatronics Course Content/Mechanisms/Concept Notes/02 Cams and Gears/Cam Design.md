## Core Ideas

- Convert continuous rotary input into a prescribed follower motion through direct contact.
- Read and construct SVAJ diagrams ($s,v,a,j$ versus cam angle $\theta$); convert to time derivatives with $\dot s = v\omega$, $\ddot s = a\omega^2$.
- Obey the fundamental law of cam design: $s,v,a$ continuous, $j$ finite (dwell $\Rightarrow v=a=0$ at the boundary).
- Use standard motion programs: cycloidal, 3-4-5 and 4-5-6-7 polynomials, and the SCCA family (modified trapezoid / modified sine).
- Size the cam with the pressure angle $\phi$, prime/base circles, and radius of curvature limits.
