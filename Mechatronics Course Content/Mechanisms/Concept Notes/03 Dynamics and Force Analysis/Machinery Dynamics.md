## Core Ideas

- Mass properties: centre of gravity of composite bodies, mass moment of inertia, and radius of gyration $k = \sqrt{I/m}$.
- Parallel-axis theorem $I_{ZZ} = I_{GG} + md^2$.
- Energy methods ($KE$, $PE$) and D'Alembert's principle ($\sum F - ma = 0$, $\sum T - I\alpha = 0$).
- Equivalent (lumped) systems: effective mass and effective spring via a lever ratio.
- Reflected inertia through a gear/lever ratio: $I_{\text{eff}} = I_{\text{load}}(\omega_{\text{load}}/\omega_{\text{ref}})^2$.
- IPS unit system: $g = 386\ \text{in/s}^2$, mass in blobs ($m = W/g$).
