## Core Ideas

- Determine joint reaction forces and the driving torque from the kinematics and inertial properties of each link.
- Newton-Euler equations per link: $\sum F_x = ma_{Gx}$, $\sum F_y = ma_{Gy}$, $\sum T_G = I_G\alpha$.
- Free-body diagrams, force notation $\mathbf{F}_{ij}$ (with $\mathbf{F}_{ji} = -\mathbf{F}_{ij}$), and $\mathbf{R}$ vectors from each CG to its joints.
- Assemble the matrix (Newtonian) method - e.g.\ a four-bar gives a $9\times9$ linear system for the joint forces and $T_{12}$.
- Use the virtual-work method as a fast route to the driving torque alone (no joint forces needed).
