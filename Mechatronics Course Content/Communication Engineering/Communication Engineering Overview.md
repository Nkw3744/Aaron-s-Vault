## 1. Foundations

- [[Introduction to Electronic Communication]] - communication systems, modulation, multiplexing, spectrum, frequency, wavelength, and bandwidth
- [[Fundamentals of Electronics and Signals]] - gain, attenuation, decibels, tuned circuits, filters, and Fourier analysis

## 2. Analogue Modulation

- [[Amplitude Modulation]] - modulation index, sidebands, power, bandwidth, and single-sideband modulation
- [[Frequency and Phase Modulation]] - FM and PM principles, sidebands, bandwidth, and noise suppression

## 3. Signal Conversion

- [[Analogue-to-Digital Conversion]] - sampling, quantisation, encoding, ADC, and DAC
- [[Pulse Modulation]] - pulse modulation, PCM, delta modulation, and sigma-delta conversion

## 4. Digital Communication

- [[Digital Modulation]] - ASK, FSK, PSK, QAM, BER, spread spectrum, and OFDM
- [[Transmitters Receivers and Noise]] - transmitter and receiver fundamentals, AWGN, noise figure, and noise temperature
- [[Information Theory]] - information, entropy, Huffman coding, and channel capacity
- [[Coding and Multiplexing]] - source, channel, and line coding; error control; multiplexing; and duplexing

## 5. Networks and Communication Systems

- [[Introduction to Computer Networks]] - OSI, TCP/IP, Internet Protocol, addressing, and subnet masks
- [[Modern Telecommunication Systems and Measurements]] - optical, cellular, satellite, wireless, SCADA, and communication testing
