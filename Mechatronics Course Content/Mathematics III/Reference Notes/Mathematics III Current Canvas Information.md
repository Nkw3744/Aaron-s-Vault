## Course structure

ENGE702 develops analytical and numerical methods for modelling engineering systems. The published sequence covers ordinary differential equations, systems and series solutions, Laplace and Fourier methods, partial differential equations, and numerical PDE work in MATLAB. Canvas describes a weekly two-hour lecture, pre-class recordings/readings, and a two-hour tutorial; tutorials are presented as an integral part of the course.
