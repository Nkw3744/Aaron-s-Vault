The roadmap connects the available ENGE702 lecture material to durable concept notes. Lecture 13 is explicitly marked as absent rather than assigning it an unverified topic.

## Current S2 2026 Canvas modules

- [Week 1](https://canvas.aut.ac.nz/courses/23164/pages/week-1-overview-s2-2026) covers modelling, simple initial-value problems, Euler's method, and direction fields. Existing Lecture 1–3 slides and L1–L3 notes were retained after exact duplicate checks.
- [Week 2](https://canvas.aut.ac.nz/courses/23164/pages/week-2-overview-s2-2026) covers direction fields, Euler's method, separable ODEs, and integrating factors. New [[L4_note.pdf|L4]], [[L5_note.pdf|L5]], and [[L6_note.pdf|L6]] handwritten notes and current tutorials are indexed locally; overlapping slide/combined-pack copies remain on Canvas.
- [Week 3](https://canvas.aut.ac.nz/courses/23164/pages/week-3-overview-s2-2026) develops second-order homogeneous ODEs through superposition, characteristic roots, general solutions, and initial conditions. The local sources are the [[Week 3 lecture 3_combined final_Canvas.pdf|combined lecture pack]] and handwritten [[L7_note.pdf|L7]], [[L8_note.pdf|L8]], and [[L9_note.pdf|L9]] notes.
