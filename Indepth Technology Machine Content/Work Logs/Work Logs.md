## Filing rule

- Original attachments retain their received filenames.
- Weekly descriptive notes use ISO start dates for reliable sorting.
- Machine-specific work is linked to the relevant machine note, while mixed-activity reports remain here rather than being filed as machine manuals.
- A time report is work evidence, not automatically a technical procedure or commissioning record.
