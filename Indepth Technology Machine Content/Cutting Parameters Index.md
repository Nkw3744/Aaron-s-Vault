## Status meanings

- **Reference:** manufacturer or third-party guidance that has not been proved on the local machine.
- **Commissioning:** controlled coupon testing is underway; settings are not released for production.
- **Validated:** the exact material, thickness, source/head/optics, nozzle, gas, CypCut settings, coupons, inspection result, revision, and approval have been recorded.
- **Superseded:** retained for traceability but replaced by a newer validated revision.

## Adding a validated recipe

For every new material, thickness, coating, or assist-gas combination:

1. Start from the machine-specific guide and preserve the known-good factory library.
2. Record the exact source, cutting head, optics, nozzle, gas supply, material grade, coating, and measured thickness.
3. Develop the recipe using labelled coupons and one-variable-at-a-time changes.
4. Repeat the selected result before release.
5. Create a separate dated recipe note or revision record and link it here.
6. Never replace a validated recipe silently; mark the previous revision as superseded.
