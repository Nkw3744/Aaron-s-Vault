# Fiber Connector Cleaning and Inspection

Return to [[QBH Fiber Delivery Cable]] · [[Technical Reference Index]]

> [!info] When to open this note
> When and how to inspect QBH interfaces before mating or after contamination event.

> [!danger] Cleanroom discipline
> A single dust particle on a high-power interface can burn into the quartz face. Work in clean area; cap connectors when open.

## When to inspect

- Before first mate at install
- After any unplanned disconnect
- After smoke/oil event near head
- If power drop or intermittent interlock
- After shipping/move — always

## Tools

- Lint-free wipes (laser grade)
- Isopropyl alcohol 99%+ or OEM fluid
- Fiber microscope 400× (service kit)
- Dust caps — never reuse dirty caps without cleaning

## QBH end face procedure (summary)

1. Disable emission; verify zero energy state
2. Remove cap; inspect under microscope if available
3. Wipe ferrule face in one direction with damp lint-free wipe
4. Dry pass with clean wipe
5. Re-inspect — no streaks, pits, or particles
6. Mate immediately or cap

Do not touch quartz block with fingers.

## Protective window (cutting head)

Separate from QBH — downstream consumable:

- Replace on schedule or when pitting/burn visible
- Clean only with approved method — often replace rather than polish in field
- Contamination from zinc, oil, or slag — [[Zn and Coated Material Fume Notes]], [[Compressed Air Cutting]]

## Contamination sources

| Source | Prevention |
| --- | --- |
| Dirty shop air cut | Filtration chain |
| Head crash slag | Nozzle hygiene |
| Condensation | [[Dew Point and Chiller Setpoints]] |
| Hand oil | Gloves; cap discipline |
